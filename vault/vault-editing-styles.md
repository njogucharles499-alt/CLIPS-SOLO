# Vault: Elite Editing Styles (5 Approaches)

Every clip fits one of 5 styles. Each style has specific must-pass principles, shot length targets, music tempo, and tactical rules.

---

## STYLE 1: FAST/ENERGY

**Best for:** Quick insights, founding announcements, hype moments, urgency

**Duration target:** 30-45 seconds (no longer)

**Must-pass principles:** Hook, Retention, Pacing, Micro-Editing

**Music tempo:** 140+ BPM (upbeat, energetic)

**Shot length target:** 1.5-2.5 seconds average (fast cuts maintain momentum)

**Cuts per 10 seconds:** 4-5 cuts (visible cadence)

### 10 Fast/Energy Rules:

1. Start mid-sentence, never with a title card or intro
2. No pauses longer than 0.5 seconds (cut dead air aggressively)
3. Every 2 seconds: new shot, new angle, or new visual moment
4. Music builds continuously (no dips or quiet moments)
5. Use text overlays to reinforce 1-2 key words only
6. B-roll moves fast: zooms, cuts, transitions allowed (show speed)
7. Reactions should be quick and snappy (not held 2+ seconds)
8. Dialogue delivered slightly faster than normal (not rushed, just energized)
9. No long establishing shots; frame-fill conversations dominate
10. End with a climactic statement or laughing reaction, hold 1 second

### Don'ts:
- DON'T use cinematic music or slow builds
- DON'T linger on any single shot
- DON'T use color grading or heavy effects (focuses on speed, not aesthetics)
- DON'T include silence or contemplative moments
- DON'T end on a whisper or quiet moment

---

## STYLE 2: CINEMATIC

**Best for:** Deep insights, strategic announcements, emotional stories, brand moments

**Duration target:** 60-90 seconds

**Must-pass principles:** Story, Emotion, Tension & Release, Silence

**Music tempo:** 80-100 BPM (instrumental, atmospheric, building)

**Shot length target:** 3-5 seconds average (breathing room for impact)

**Cuts per 10 seconds:** 2-3 cuts (deliberate, not rushed)

### 10 Cinematic Rules:

1. Open with wide shot or context-setting moment (establish the world)
2. Use color grading to set mood (cooler = serious, warmer = hopeful)
3. Include 1-3 B-roll sequences that prove the claim (not random decoration)
4. Build tension in middle: problem gets bigger, stakes increase
5. Release tension at peak: solution/insight lands with clarity
6. Use silence strategically: 1-2 second pause after a major statement
7. Match music to emotional arc: quiet intro → builds → peaks → resolves
8. Hold reaction shots 2-3+ seconds (let emotion breathe)
9. Use smooth transitions between ideas (not hard cuts)
10. End with Anton's face or a powerful callback to the opening

### Don'ts:
- DON'T use fast cuts or hyperactive pacing
- DON'T include music with lyrics or speech-like elements
- DON'T forget B-roll (it's essential for cinematic)
- DON'T skip the color grading pass
- DON'T end abruptly (always finish with grace)

**Note:** Cinematic clips require **Premiere export for music/color grading finishing** if those elements are specified. OpusClip can provide the base edit, but final music and grading happen in Premiere.

## STYLE 3: COMEDY

**Best for:** Funny moments, absurd contrasts, self-aware humor, relatable struggles

**Duration target:** 30-60 seconds

**Must-pass principles:** Rhythm, Reactions, Silence, Payoff

**Music tempo:** 110-130 BPM (playful, bouncy)

**Shot length target:** 2-3.5 seconds average (pacing serves punchlines)

**Cuts per 10 seconds:** 3-4 cuts (snappy without frantic)

### 10 Comedy Rules:

1. Hook with the setup of the joke, not the punchline
2. Build anticipation before the punchline (don't reveal too early)
3. On the punchline: cut to Anton's face, reaction, or visual payoff
4. Hold the reaction 1-2 seconds AFTER the laugh peaks (let it land)
5. Use silence 0.5-1.5 seconds after punchline (let audience laugh internally)
6. Never auto-remove "um"s or stutters if they're part of the humor
7. Keep natural authenticity: real laughs beat perfectly-timed fake reactions
8. Use music to build anticipation (ramp up before joke, drop after)
9. Include cutaway reactions (co-host, audience) if available
10. End on the biggest laugh or a callback punchline (leave them smiling)

### Don'ts:
- DON'T remove the setup (joke needs context)
- DON'T cut away from Anton's reaction (that's where the laugh is)
- DON'T use music with heavy emotional tone (undercuts humor)
- DON'T auto-remove pauses (pauses ARE the comedic timing)
- DON'T explain the joke with on-screen text

## STYLE 4: EDUCATIONAL

**Best for:** How-to moments, explanations, multi-step processes, teaching insights

**Duration target:** 45-90 seconds

**Must-pass principles:** Information Hierarchy, Pacing, Clarity

**Music tempo:** 90-110 BPM (steady, supporting, not distracting)

**Shot length target:** 3-4 seconds average (time to absorb information)

**Cuts per 10 seconds:** 2-3 cuts (measured, not rushed)

### 10 Educational Rules:

1. Lead with the core learning (what will they know in 10 seconds)
2. Structure as: Concept → Example → Application → Takeaway
3. Use B-roll to illustrate each concept (show as you explain)
4. Add on-screen text for key terms or statistics (reinforce learning)
5. Pause 1-2 seconds after major concepts (let it sink in)
6. Use graphics or charts if explaining data (make invisible visible)
7. Speak slightly slower than normal conversational pace (clear articulation)
8. Include Anton's teaching moments: hand gestures, pointing, walking through logic
9. End with a memorable takeaway or summary statement
10. Repeat the main point in different words (reinforce learning)

### Don'ts:
- DON'T assume prior knowledge (always provide context)
- DON'T use fast cuts or hyperactive pacing (it's distracting)
- DON'T include jargon without definition
- DON'T forget to show what you're explaining (B-roll is essential)
- DON'T end without a clear takeaway (what did they just learn?)

---

## STYLE 5: INSPIRATIONAL

**Best for:** Big-vision moments, calls to action, future-forward statements, belief shifts

**Duration target:** 60-90 seconds

**Must-pass principles:** Story, Emotion, Tension & Release, Payoff

**Music tempo:** 100-120 BPM (building, uplifting, hopeful)

**Shot length target:** 3-5 seconds average (room for emotion to land)

**Cuts per 10 seconds:** 2-3 cuts (purposeful, not rushed)

### 10 Inspirational Rules:

1. Start with the problem or status quo (what's broken today)
2. Build tension: show why this matters, what's at stake
3. Introduce the vision: "Here's what could be instead"
4. Use B-roll to show the future state (aspirational imagery)
5. Include Anton's emotional conviction (sincerity, not performative)
6. Build music intensity as vision expands (crescendo at core message)
7. Use silence moments to emphasize major revelations
8. Hold reactions and key frames 2-3+ seconds (let hope breathe)
9. End with a call to action or reframed belief
10. Close with Anton's face showing quiet confidence or resolve

### Don'ts:
- DON'T use cynical tone or undercut the message
- DON'T skip the emotional build (inspiration needs arc)
- DON'T use upbeat music if the message is serious (tone mismatch)
- DON'T forget B-roll (show the vision, don't just talk about it)
- DON'T end on a whisper (end bold, even if quiet)

**Note:** Inspirational clips require **Premiere export for music/color grading finishing** if those elements are specified. OpusClip can provide the base edit, but final music and grading happen in Premiere.
