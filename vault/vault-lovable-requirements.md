# Vault: Lovable Content Rewards Campaign Requirements

These are the 7 non-negotiable rules from the official campaign brief. Violate ANY of these and the clip will be rejected.

## REQUIREMENT 1: Majority Tier-1 Viewership
Your account (@prime.clips or your channel) must have audience concentrated in Tier-1 countries.

**Tier-1 countries:** USA, Canada, UK, Australia, Western Europe, Japan, South Korea, etc.

**What Claude checks:** TikTok audience analytics. If you have 60%+ Tier-1 viewers, you pass.

**Your action:** Keep TikTok's "Original language" set to English (already done). This protects Tier-1 positioning.

**Red flag:** If your audience is mostly Tier-2/3, Content Rewards may reject submissions later even if they pass other checks.

The clip MUST show and focus on either:
- **Anton** (Lovable founder/CEO), OR

**Approved podcast sources** (examples):

Your account must be ONE of:

**NOT required:** Account must be literally brand-new (you can use an older account if it's AI/startup-focused)

## REQUIREMENT 4: #LovablePartner Hashtag (Caption Field ONLY)
**MUST include** `#LovablePartner` in the video caption (the "Describe your video" text field on TikTok).

**Critical detail:** This is a caption field hashtag, NOT on-screen text overlay.

**Where to place it:**
- TikTok: "Describe your video" box → include #LovablePartner somewhere in the text
- OpusClip: "Regenerate copy" caption box → include #LovablePartner
- YouTube: Description field → include #LovablePartner at the end

**What happens if you forget:**
- First clip submitted without hashtag: Content Rewards may still approve if other rules pass (ask in Discord)
- Subsequent clips without hashtag: Likely rejection

**Your mistake (Clip #1):** Posted without #LovablePartner. You're waiting to hear if it still counts. Use it for every clip from #2 onward.

## REQUIREMENT 5: Comments Permanently ON + At Least 1 Comment
Comments must be enabled on your video, and you must have at least 1 comment (can be your own).

**Your action:**
- When posting to TikTok, allow comments (default is usually ON)
- Leave one of your own comments if needed (just to guarantee the count)
- Do NOT turn comments off

**Why:** Lovable wants engagement and community signal.

## REQUIREMENT 6: Submit to Content Rewards Within 10 Minutes of Posting
You must submit the video link to the Lovable Content Rewards dashboard within 10 minutes of posting it to TikTok/YouTube.

**How:**
- Post to TikTok or YouTube
- Copy the video URL
- Go to Content Rewards dashboard (Whop)
- Click "Submit" and paste the link
- Submit within 10-minute window

**If you miss the 10 minutes:**
- Submission may still process but might be flagged
- Avoid cutting it close; submit immediately after posting

**Your current issue:** Submissions failing silently (button spins, no success/error message). You've submitted support tickets. Wait for their response.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: Submission failing silently?**
A: File a support ticket on the Whop dashboard. Ask in Discord. Wait patiently over weekend.

**Q: Clip #1 didn't have #LovablePartner. Does it still count?**
A: Ask in Discord. Most likely: it'll still count IF other requirements are met, but no guarantees. Focus on getting #2 right.

**Q: How long until I see payment?**
A: Auto-approved clips are usually paid within 48 hours. Manual review can take weeks. Payment method: Whop (usually PayPal or bank transfer).

**Q: What if a clip is rejected?**
A: Check the rejection reason in Whop. Most common: not from approved podcast, doesn't feature Anton clearly enough, no tier-1 audience. Adjust and resubmit new clip.
