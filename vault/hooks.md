# Hook Types

A clip's first 3 seconds determine whether a viewer scrolls past or stays. Every selected clip must open with one of the following 5 hook types. Identify the hook type before finalising the cut point.

---

## Hook 1 — The Bold Claim

**Definition:** An unqualified, provocative statement that challenges a widely held assumption.

**Pattern:** `"[Thing everyone believes] is wrong / dead / a lie."`

**Examples:**
- "College is the worst investment most people will ever make."
- "Everything you know about productivity is backwards."
- "The advice that made me rich would have destroyed me five years ago."

**Cut guidance:** Start the clip at the first syllable of the claim. Do not include lead-in phrases ("so I was thinking…", "the thing is…"). Trim ruthlessly to the moment the claim lands.

**Why it works:** Pattern interrupts the default-agree loop in the viewer's brain. Forces a stop-scroll reflex.

---

## Hook 2 — The Open Loop

**Definition:** A question or half-revealed story that the viewer must watch to resolve.

**Pattern:** `"What happened next changed everything."` / `"Here's what nobody tells you about X."`

**Examples:**
- "I gave away $1 million. Here's what happened."
- "She asked me one question and I couldn't answer it."
- "There's a reason the best founders never talk about this."

**Cut guidance:** End the hook at the moment of maximum tension — do not resolve the loop in the first 3 seconds. The resolution must arrive by the 20-second mark or viewer retention collapses.

**Why it works:** The Zeigarnik effect — unfinished tasks occupy working memory until closed.

---

## Hook 3 — The Empathy Mirror

**Definition:** Directly names a pain point, frustration, or desire the target viewer experiences daily.

**Pattern:** `"If you've ever felt [specific pain], this is for you."`

**Examples:**
- "If you've ever launched something and heard nothing — I've been there."
- "Tired of building things nobody uses? That was me for three years."
- "You're not lazy. You're building in the wrong direction."

**Cut guidance:** The named pain must be hyper-specific — generic pain ("feeling stuck") has no hook power. If the speaker generalises, cut to the first moment they get specific.

**Why it works:** Identity resonance. The viewer feels seen, which creates an instant parasocial bond.

---

## Hook 4 — The Counterintuitive Insight

**Definition:** A finding or conclusion that is the opposite of what conventional wisdom predicts.

**Pattern:** `"The [expected thing] actually makes [outcome] worse."`

**Examples:**
- "The more options you give customers, the fewer they buy."
- "Working harder on the wrong problem is how startups die slowly."
- "Raising prices saved this company when discounting nearly killed it."

**Cut guidance:** Lead with the conclusion, not the data. The explanation follows in the body of the clip. Never open with "studies show" or "research suggests" — that's a scroll trigger.

**Why it works:** Violates prediction error. The brain prioritises resolving surprise over everything else.

---

## Hook 5 — The High-Stakes Moment

**Definition:** Opens in the middle of a dramatic moment — an argument, a decision, a failure, a confession.

**Pattern:** In media res. No setup, no context. Pure scene.

**Examples:**
- "I had 48 hours to save the company or shut it down."
- [Clip starts with voice cracking] "I almost quit that day."
- "The investor said no. That was the best thing that ever happened to us."

**Cut guidance:** Cut to the highest-energy frame in the transcript — look for vocal stress peaks, pauses before revelation, or the moment the speaker's composure breaks. Do not add context before the hook; drop the viewer into the scene cold.

**Why it works:** Mirrors the narrative grammar of prestige TV. The viewer assumes they've missed something important and rewinds.

---

## Hook Scoring

Before finalising any clip, score its opening 3 seconds:

| Criterion                        | Pass |
|----------------------------------|------|
| Hook type clearly identifiable?  | Y/N  |
| Resolution or payoff exists?     | Y/N  |
| No filler words in first 3 s?    | Y/N  |
| Energy level high at open?       | Y/N  |

A clip that scores 3/4 or lower on hook criteria is cut from the shortlist.
