# Vault: Hook Types for Lovable Clips

A hook is the first 3-5 seconds that convince the viewer to keep watching.

## Hook Type 1: Curiosity
**How it works:** Create an information gap — the viewer wants the missing answer.

**Example phrases:**
- "What I discovered surprised everyone..."
- "This one change made all the difference..."
- "You won't believe what happened next..."

**In Lovable context:**
- Anton: "No-code will replace 90% of software engineers... but not in the way you think."
- Frame it as mysterious but promise immediate payoff
- Works best when the payoff lands within 20 seconds

**How to edit:** 
- Start mid-sentence for urgency
- Hold Anton's face as he delivers the hook
- Slight zoom in to emphasize importance
- No B-roll during hook — keep focus on him

---

## Hook Type 2: Contrast
**How it works:** Juxtapose two opposing ideas; the tension pulls the viewer in.

**Example patterns:**
- "Everyone thinks X... but actually Y"
- "Most people do X... the best do Y"
- "This looks like X... but it's actually Y"

**In Lovable context:**
- Anton: "Building software used to take 10 engineers. Now one person can do it in Lovable."
- Show the contrast: traditional dev workflow → Lovable speed
- Visual contrast too: complexity → simplicity

**How to edit:**
- First half: traditional/slow/hard (darker visuals if possible)
- Hard cut at the "but"
- Second half: Lovable/fast/easy (bright visuals)
- Music change can emphasize the shift

---

## Hook Type 3: Revelation / Insight
**How it works:** A unexpected truth that reframes how the viewer thinks.

**Example patterns:**
- "The real reason people fail is..."
- "What nobody talks about is..."
- "The secret ingredient is..."

**In Lovable context:**
- Anton: "Most founder failures aren't about product. They're about operations and hiring. Lovable solves one piece of that."
- Feels authoritative and insider knowledge
- Works well with statistics or examples

**How to edit:**
- Start with Anton's face in close-up
- Deliver the insight clearly, one sentence
- Optional: B-roll of AI/code/building during delivery
- Hold the final 1-2 seconds of silence after he finishes

---

## Hook Type 4: Stakes / Urgency
**How it works:** Imply that something important, rare, or time-limited is at stake.

**Example patterns:**
- "If you don't understand this, you'll be left behind..."
- "This window won't stay open forever..."
- "This changes everything about how we build..."

**In Lovable context:**
- Anton: "In 5 years, the companies building with AI will have 100x the speed of traditional companies."
- Creates FOMO (fear of missing out)
- Motivates action

**How to edit:**
- Deliver with urgency (slight increase in delivery speed)
- No dead air or pauses
- Music can gradually increase in intensity
- Optional: flashy B-roll of other companies/founders building

---

## Hook Type 5: Reaction / Emotion
**How it works:** A surprising reaction, laugh, or emotional beat that's contagious.

**Example patterns:**
- Someone laughs at an absurd idea
- Disbelief reaction to a stat
- Frustration that becomes relief
- Joy at a breakthrough

**In Lovable context:**
- Anton reacts with genuine surprise: "Wait, you built that entire workflow in 20 minutes?!"
- Authentic reactions resonate more than prepared statements
- Can be Anton's reaction OR co-host's reaction to what Anton says

**How to edit:**
- Lead with the setup (what triggers the reaction)
- Isolate the reaction moment (no cutaways)
- Option: show reaction shot + B-roll of what caused it
- Pause just after the reaction lands (let it breathe)

---

## Scoring Moments

When you scan the transcript, rate each candidate moment:

**Curiosity:** 1-10 scale. Does it make me want to know more?
**Contrast:** 1-10 scale. Does it flip an assumption?
**Insight:** 1-10 scale. Does it feel true and non-obvious?
**Stakes:** 1-10 scale. Does it make me feel urgency?
**Reaction:** 1-10 scale. Is the emotional beat authentic and contagious?

**Top candidates:** Scores of 7+ across any dimension.

Use the highest-scoring moment as your hook.
