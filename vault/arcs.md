# Emotional Arc Templates

Every clip must follow one of the three arc templates below. Assign the arc before finalising cut points — the arc determines where the clip begins, where it peaks, and where it ends. A clip that does not fit any arc cleanly is not a clip; it is a fragment.

---

## Arc 1 — Fall & Rise (Redemption)

**Shape:** Pain → Lowest Point → Turning Point → Resolution

**Emotional journey:** Vulnerability → Despair → Insight → Hope

**Ideal clip duration:** 60–90 seconds

**Structure:**

```
0:00–0:10   Hook — name the fall (Hook types 3 or 5)
0:10–0:40   Descent — specific details of the failure or pain
0:40–0:60   Turning point — the moment everything changed
0:60–0:90   Resolution — what is true now / the lesson
```

**Transcript markers to look for:**
- "I hit rock bottom when…"
- "I was about to give up / quit / shut it down…"
- "And then something shifted…"
- "Now I…" / "Today…" / "That's when I realised…"

**Editing notes:**
- The descent must feel real — do not cut the discomfort short.
- The turning point is the emotional peak; this is where the music swell or silence lands.
- The resolution should be 1–2 sentences maximum. Longer = preachy.
- Do not end on advice. End on emotion.

**Platform fit:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts

---

## Arc 2 — Insight Bomb (Revelation)

**Shape:** Conventional belief → Challenge → Proof → Reframe

**Emotional journey:** Comfort → Disruption → Curiosity → Expansion

**Ideal clip duration:** 30–60 seconds

**Structure:**

```
0:00–0:05   Hook — state the counterintuitive claim (Hook types 1 or 4)
0:05–0:25   Challenge — why the common belief is wrong
0:25–0:45   Proof — one concrete example or data point
0:45–0:60   Reframe — what to believe / do instead
```

**Transcript markers to look for:**
- "Everyone thinks X, but…"
- "The data actually shows…"
- "What most people don't realise is…"
- "The real reason is…"
- "So instead of X, try Y."

**Editing notes:**
- Speed matters. Every second of this arc must earn its place.
- The proof section must be concrete — a number, a name, a specific story.
- Cut abstract filler ("it's kind of like…", "in a sense…") without mercy.
- The reframe is the clip's value proposition. Never bury it.

**Platform fit:** Twitter/X, LinkedIn clips, YouTube Shorts

---

## Arc 3 — The Stakes Climb (Tension)

**Shape:** Normal → Complication → Escalation → Cliffhanger / Release

**Emotional journey:** Curiosity → Anxiety → Suspense → Satisfaction or Dread

**Ideal clip duration:** 45–90 seconds

**Structure:**

```
0:00–0:08   Hook — drop into the scene mid-action (Hook type 2 or 5)
0:08–0:30   Complication — what went wrong / what was at risk
0:30–0:60   Escalation — the stakes get higher; the window closes
0:60–0:90   Release — resolution (or deliberate cliffhanger for multi-part)
```

**Transcript markers to look for:**
- "We had 24 hours…" / "By Friday…" / "The deadline was…"
- "I didn't know if we'd make it."
- "Every option was off the table except one."
- "We had one shot."
- "I've never told anyone this…"

**Editing notes:**
- Pacing is the primary tool — cut faster as stakes escalate.
- Use silence at the moment of maximum tension. Do not fill it with music.
- A deliberate cliffhanger ending ("find out in Part 2") is valid only if Part 2 is already queued.
- Never resolve the tension too early — hold the release until the final 10 seconds.

**Platform fit:** TikTok series, Instagram carousel-to-Reel, YouTube long-form excerpts

---

## Arc Assignment Checklist

Before locking a clip, complete this checklist:

| Question                                      | Answer |
|-----------------------------------------------|--------|
| Which arc does this clip follow?               |        |
| Is the hook in the correct position (0–3 s)?   | Y/N    |
| Does the arc peak appear? (timestamp)          |        |
| Does the clip end at the right emotional beat? | Y/N    |
| Does the clip fit the arc's ideal duration?    | Y/N    |

A clip without a confirmed arc assignment does not advance to editing.
