# Vault: Emotional Arc Templates

Every clip follows an emotional journey. These are the three most common for Lovable.

## Arc 1: SHORT-CLIP (30-45 seconds)

**Structure:** Hook → Insight → Payoff

**Timeline:**
- 0-3s: Hook (what makes you stay)
- 3-30s: Insight/Build (the substance)
- 30-45s: Payoff/Tag (the takeaway or laugh)

**Example: Anton on No-Code**
- 0-3s: "Everyone thinks no-code is just templates..."
- 3-30s: "But Lovable actually lets non-technical founders build real software..."
- 30-45s: "Which means they can compete with full dev teams."

**Emotional rhythm:**
- Curiosity → understanding → confidence

**Editing style:**
- Fast cuts
- No lingering shots
- Music progresses throughout
- Text overlay for emphasis (1-2 words max per line)

**Best for:** Making one clear point fast.

---

## Arc 2: MID-LENGTH (45-60 seconds)

**Structure:** Setup → Conflict → Resolution → Insight

**Timeline:**
- 0-5s: Setup (context, what we're discussing)
- 5-25s: Conflict (the problem, the tension)
- 25-50s: Resolution (how Lovable solves it)
- 50-60s: Insight (why this matters)

**Example: Anton on Founder Speed**
- 0-5s: "Hiring is the biggest bottleneck for startups..."
- 5-25s: "Most founders spend 6 months hiring their first engineer..."
- 25-50s: "With Lovable, they can ship product in weeks while they build the team..."
- 50-60s: "Speed is the new moat."

**Emotional rhythm:**
- Tension → relief → insight

**Editing style:**
- Medium pacing
- Can use B-roll to illustrate problems
- Music builds, then resolves
- Allow 1-2 breathing moments

**Best for:** Telling a short story that explains Lovable's value.

---

## Arc 3: LONG-FORM (60-90 seconds)

**Structure:** Hook → Context → Escalation → Peak → Resolution

**Timeline:**
- 0-5s: Hook (grab attention immediately)
- 5-20s: Context (what are we talking about)
- 20-45s: Escalation (the stakes get higher, problem deepens)
- 45-75s: Peak (the turning point, the moment)
- 75-90s: Resolution (what it means now)

**Example: Anton on AI-First Companies**
- 0-5s: "Traditional software companies are already obsolete."
- 5-20s: "Here's why: they're built for static software..."
- 20-45s: "But AI systems improve daily. They're adaptive. Iterative. Better tomorrow than today..."
- 45-75s: "By the time a traditional company ships 2.0, an AI company has shipped 50 iterations..."
- 75-90s: "The race is over. AI companies won. The question is: are you building one?"

**Emotional rhythm:**
- Shock → understanding → urgency → hope → clarity

**Editing style:**
- Variable pacing (fast sections, then slow)
- Multiple visual layers (A-roll + B-roll + graphics)
- Music dynamic (quiet moments of realization, then builds)
- Silence used strategically (after the peak moment)

**Best for:** Deep insights, controversial takes, stories with emotional weight.

---

## How to Choose Your Arc

Ask these questions:

1. **What's the core message?** (One sentence)
2. **How much time do I have to explain it?** (30-45s, 45-60s, or 60-90s)
3. **How many emotional beats should I land?** (1-2 for short, 2-3 for mid, 4-5 for long)
4. **Does this need story context or is it instant impact?** (Instant = Short, needs setup = Mid or Long)

### Example Decision Tree

**Message:** "Lovable saves founders 6 months of hiring"
- Can explain in one fact → **SHORT** (30-45s)
- Need to show problem + solution → **MID** (45-60s)
- Want to show the competitive advantage → **LONG** (60-90s)
